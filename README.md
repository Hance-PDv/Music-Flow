# 🎵 Music Flow

Music Flow is a lightweight, modern, and ad-free Android music player designed for local audio playback with advanced features and a clean user experience.

---

## ✨ Features
* 🎧 **Local Audio Playback**: Smooth and fast local media scanning.
* 📜 **Synced Lyrics**: Embedded and synchronized lyrics support.
* 📊 **Listening Statistics**: Track your most played songs and habits.
* ⏱️ **Sleep Timer**: Auto-pause playback at your scheduled time.
* ❤️ **Favorites & Playlists**: Organize your local music library effortlessly.
* 🎨 **Clean Dark UI**: Modern dark theme designed for OLED displays.
